Arbiter: signal flow and wiring

One radio, several programs (Subspace, fldigi, VarAC with VARA) through flrig. The Arbiter is a helper beside flrig. These two pictures show what talks to what, and what is actually wired to what. Arbiter 1.0.1, WM8Q, 2026-10-29.

Whatever is already transmitting finishes. The Arbiter holds only what has not started, by throwing each program's own switch, and it never refuses, swallows or delays a PTT.

normal CAT traffic (every program as today) the Arbiter sees (read only) the Arbiter holds (the program's own switch)

3. Wiring: what is actually connected to what

Cables and port numbers. Only three things reach the radio: one audio path, one CAT line, one antenna. Nothing of the Arbiter's touches any of them.

4. Reading the wiring

  • Only three things reach the radio: the audio (one sound device, shared by every program through the operating system's mixer), the serial/USB CAT line (owned by flrig alone), and the antenna. The Arbiter has none of them.
  • flrig is the only program on the serial line. Every program keys and tunes by asking flrig on 12345. With CAT Watch in front, flrig moves to 12346 and CAT Watch answers 12345; no program notices.
  • The Arbiter has four wires, all inside the PC: 12345 to flrig (reads PTT and the dial, never keys), 2442 to Subspace, 7362 to fldigi, and VarAC's two files. None runs through another program's path, so unplugging the Arbiter changes nothing above it.
  • VarAC has no port the Arbiter could use: VARA's 8300 and 8301 belong to VarAC alone, which is why VarAC is read and held through its own files.
  • Audio is where collisions become real: two programs keying at once put two signals into one audio channel on one keyed radio. That is what the holds prevent; the audio wiring is never changed.

6. Limits worth knowing

  • One poll (200 ms) of reaction time: a program that keys inside that window collides as it does today. No frame is ever lost to a refusal, because there is none.
  • Keying that bypasses flrig (VOX, a separate PTT line) is invisible to the Arbiter.
  • Subspace and VarAC release themselves if the Arbiter dies (300 s lease, 30 s keepalive). fldigi's stock switch has no expiry: a hard kill (not a close) while holding fldigi leaves it receive-only until fldigi is restarted.
  • Do not stop and restart the Arbiter while it is holding: a stop releases everything.
